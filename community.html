Welcome to the Class of '86 digital hangout

This is the quiet corner of the internet for our class. Unlock the gateway, look up a name, flip through the 25th and 40th photos, and write in when you have news. No app to download. No passwords to remember besides the one classmate code.

Reunion committee

The 40th was a gift

We had such a good time that we're not waiting around until the 50-year mark to do it again. When a date and place are locked in, they'll replace the "to be announced" lines on this page. Watch here, and tell a classmate who doesn't live online.

Write in Send a throwback photo Hallway shots, football games, prom, the parking lot. Grainy is better.

Photos go to the committee. They'll add them to the Memories board so they stay with the class, not on one person's phone.
